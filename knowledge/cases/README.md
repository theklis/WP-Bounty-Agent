# Historical case studies

Store **CURATED**, sanitized educational accounts of particular confirmed or rejected paths, unusual architectures, and important audit misses. Preserve the actors, objects, trust boundary, decisive human outcome, successful or failed chain premises, and what must not be generalized. A case may be useful without supporting a cross-target pattern. Use the [case template](../../templates/case.md) and [learning workflow](../../docs/learning-workflow.md); keep raw records and private evidence in ignored `research/lessons/`.

Public target/version attribution may be retained deliberately after provenance, licensing, and disclosure review. Do not publish private source, client data, secrets, or raw PoCs. A sanitized benchmark-derived case may be curated only after its blind run is frozen and human review approves it; later same-target runs are known-answer regressions.
