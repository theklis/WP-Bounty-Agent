# Learning model

The repository accumulates reviewed reusable knowledge; writing files does not retrain the underlying model. A human research outcome or correction is the basis for outcome-derived learning. The agent may **PROPOSE** a lesson, but may not silently **CURATE** it. Follow the [LEARN FROM RESULT workflow](learning-workflow.md); keep raw feedback, private evidence, and proposals in ignored `research/lessons/` until reviewed.

## Evidence and scope

Record the source outcome before deriving a lesson: a human-confirmed finding, a tested rejected hypothesis, a partially correct path, a researcher correction, a missed known vulnerability, or a careful no-finding result. Preserve the audit/finding/case ID, target and version where applicable, date, conditions, actual human result, and links to local [finding](findings.md) or [journal](../templates/journal.md) records. A brief statement such as “confirmed” does not supply an exploit chain, affected versions, root cause, or impact by itself; leave unknown details open rather than inventing them.

Retain the approved basis categories: **verified fact**, **researcher-provided feedback**, **inferred pattern**, and **experimental technique**. A lesson may cite more than one basis, with each claim tied to its supporting evidence. Human feedback is valuable but does not automatically prove a broad rule; an inference or untested technique must not masquerade as a verified fact. Separate a target-specific fact, reusable observation, research heuristic, proposed method change, and permanent platform semantic. Platform-wide claims need adequate platform-level sources, not one plugin's behavior.

A rejection shows only that the tested path failed under recorded conditions; it does not establish that similar patterns, APIs, or attack paths are safe elsewhere. One confirmed vulnerability does not establish a universal exploit rule either. Record applicability, necessary preconditions, known counterexamples, what would defeat the path, and why any generalization is justified. One well-understood case plus sound platform reasoning can support a bounded heuristic; several occurrences do not make a rule automatically. For multi-stage paths, retain which primitive worked, what authority or state it produced, which consumer used it, and where the bridge succeeded or failed. A technically valid result that is outside current bounty scope remains technically valid; changing program rules, CVE assignment, and historical severity do not become timeless security doctrine.

## Curated knowledge

Use [cases](../knowledge/cases/README.md) for sanitized, educational target-specific paths, including attractive false positives or important misses; [patterns](../knowledge/patterns/README.md) for bounded, falsifiable cross-target research patterns; and [lessons](../knowledge/lessons/README.md) for operational research, prioritization, and falsification guidance. Do not duplicate a full case across all three. A curated reusable lesson should say when it applies, what to trace or check, why the source outcome mattered, what would defeat the hypothesis, and what must not be generalized. Use the [lesson template](../templates/lesson.md) to make the basis and scope reviewable.

Curated records require provenance, sensitivity review, and explicit human approval. Remove private client names, domains, addresses, credentials, tokens, raw proof artifacts, proprietary source, and business data while preserving the useful technical structure. Public target/version attribution may remain when deliberate and appropriate. Check licensing and disclosure constraints before including public source excerpts. Link a public source or an approved sanitized local reference without exposing ignored material. A proposal is not authoritative audit guidance. Load **CURATED** records selectively by target architecture, feature, chosen playbook, or research question; consult proposals only while reviewing learning.

Contradictory evidence calls for investigation, not silent replacement. Link the new outcome, examine versions and preconditions, narrow the old scope or mark it **SUPERSEDED** with a replacement link, and preserve its history. Changes to stable methodology or specialist playbooks require separate approved change candidates; curation alone does not edit those documents. Git history should expose deliberate revisions to curated knowledge.
