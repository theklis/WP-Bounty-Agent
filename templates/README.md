# Record templates

Copy these into the ignored local `research/` workspace when needed. They define fields, not completed research or executable tooling.

- [Finding](finding.md): status, chain, evidence, uncertainty, human validation.
- [Research journal](journal.md): dated audit decisions, dead ends, and coverage.
- [Lesson](lesson.md): outcome, provenance, generalization, review.
- [Current intelligence](intelligence-record.md): dated program or CVE facts.

Delete prompts that do not apply, but do not erase unknown or contradictory premises merely to make a record look complete.
